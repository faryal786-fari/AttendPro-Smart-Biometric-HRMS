About the Institution

A tradition of training, discipline and public service.

Police Training College Lahore is a Punjab Police training institution. This page highlights its Campus II at Manawan. Punjab Police records state that Campus II was first established near Thokar Niaz Baig in June 2004 to meet training needs of the Punjab Highway Patrol, and was shifted to the KC Factory site on G.T. Road, Manawan, in June 2006.

2004Campus II established near Thokar Niaz Baig
2006Campus II shifted to Manawan
2012Police Training School Lahore designated as Police College Lahore

Location clarification: Thokar Niaz Baig is part of the Campus II history. The current Campus II location described by Punjab Police is G.T. Road, Manawan, Lahore.

System Users
Simple roles: View Only, Supervisor, and Super Admin.

👁 View Only User

Can view the dashboard and attendance, and export or print reports. Cannot edit attendance, change device settings, or manage users.

🧑‍💼 Supervisor

Includes all View Only permissions, plus attendance check-in/check-out and the ability to review and update attendance records.

🛡 Super Admin

Full access: add, switch, configure, and sync devices; create, update, and delete users; edit attendance; and manage reports.
